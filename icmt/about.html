Institute for Computer Music and Technology

ICMT

The Institute for Computer Music and Technology (ICMT) represents an interdisciplinary research environment connecting music, computing, artificial intelligence, and interactive technology. My work explores how computational systems can become practical tools for musical creation, learning, and performance.

Research

AI Music Generation

Exploring neural and stochastic approaches to symbolic music generation, including LSTM and retrieval-based methods.

Jazz Improvisation

Developing computational approaches for jazz lick generation, improvisation learning, and melodic idea generation.

Web-based Music Systems

Designing browser-based musical applications using JavaScript, Web Audio, APIs, and interactive interfaces.

Interactive Music Technology

Building systems that connect musicians, instruments, software, sensors, and real-time sound processing.

Research Approach

My research focuses on the relationship between musical practice and computational technology. Rather than treating AI as a replacement for the musician, I explore systems that generate ideas, provide interactive possibilities, and support musicians in learning and creating music.

Selected Projects

BebopBot

A web-based jazz lick generator using an LSTM model. The system generates short melodic ideas that musicians can listen to, practice, memorize, and apply to improvisation.

LSTM Jazz AI Music Web Audio

Stochastic Sequence Generation

A study comparing LSTM and retrieval-based approaches for generating melodic sequences from jazz data, with emphasis on melodic contour and interval relationships.

LSTM RAG Music Generation WJazzD

DrSax.js

A JavaScript-based Web Audio library developed for browser-based saxophone and musical instrument applications.

JavaScript Web Audio API Sound Synthesis

Webxophone

A web-based wind instrument system exploring touch interaction, motion sensing, sound synthesis, and musical expression.
