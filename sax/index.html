01 / 06

EUY SHICK HONG IS A JAZZ SAXOPHONIST, COMPUTER MUSICIAN, RESEARCHER, AND MUSIC TECHNOLOGIST.

Dr. Hong studied jazz saxophone at Delta Community Music School in Canada and received a Doctor of Musical Arts (D.M.A.) in Computer Music from Dongguk University.

He also holds a Master of Computer Engineering (M.C.E.) from Yonsei University and a Master of Music (M.M.) in Computer Music from Dongguk University.

He was invited as a Visiting Scholar to Plymouth University in the UK and has presented work on music technology at Carnegie Mellon University.

His musical work combines jazz, blues, funky, pop, contemporary music, interactive performance, and computer music technology.
